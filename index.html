About

Growing up across different countries gave me an early instinct for how systems — economic, political, financial, and institutional — shape the choices available to people and organisations. This sparked a lifelong curiosity about how complex systems work: how to take them apart, understand what drives them, and think about how they might be put back together more effectively.

My path has taken me from audit at Ernst & Young and hospitality to academic research at UCL and consulting — giving me a practical grounding for a non-linear path.

I'm currently an Associate at BCG Expand, working on benchmarking and data analysis. Alongside this, I'm co-writing a paper with Dr Aparna Ravi at UCL that extends my dissertation research on manufacturing, structural transformation and global trade. My broader research interest is how technological change propagates through trade structures, asset prices and institutions.
